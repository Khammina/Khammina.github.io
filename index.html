01 — About

Engineering precision, delivered end to end

I am an MEPF technical engineer and shop drawing draftsman with six years of site and design-office experience across hotel, industrial, commercial and private residential projects in Laos.

My core work is the preparation and coordination of shop drawings and technical drawings for mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire-protection and LPG systems. I take drawings through the full project lifecycle — concept and permit sets, coordinated construction issues, technical submittals, and as-built documentation — resolving clashes with architectural and structural disciplines before they reach the site.

On site I supervise installation teams, verify that work follows the approved drawings, and carry out testing and commissioning of distribution boards, lighting, plumbing fixtures and LPG supply prior to handover.

Alongside engineering, I develop rule-based Expert Advisers for XAU (gold) and Forex markets on MetaTrader, and design brand and front-end material — work that keeps my quantitative and visual skills sharp and directly informs how I document and present technical information.
